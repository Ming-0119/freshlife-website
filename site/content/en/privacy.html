Privacy Policy

This page describes the iOS version. For the web app, see browser storage and backup information (Chinese). For Android 1.0.1, see the Android privacy policy (Chinese) and Android support guide (Chinese).

FreshLife believes the everyday details of your household don’t need to leave your device. This policy tries to say, in plain language: what stays on your device, when anything ever connects, and what you can do at any time.

Last updated: August 2026Applies to the FreshLife iOS app (current version 1.0.0)

1. The one-sentence version

The current version of FreshLife requires no account and has no cloud sync. Core data like your pantry and preferences stays on your device by default; there are no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking. If optional remote AI ships later, only the minimum needed for a request would be processed, and only when you have turned it on and use it.

2. Data stored on your device

FreshLife stores the following locally on your device — it is not uploaded anywhere automatically:

  • Item records: name, category, storage spot, expiry date, quantity, unit, notes and optional photos;
  • Food history: when items were added, how much was used, whether anything was discarded;
  • Completed meals and shopping list entries;
  • Preference and onboarding settings: goals, eating style, liked and avoided foods, allergy information;
  • An optional profile: nickname, avatar, gender, birthday, region and bio (not required for core features);
  • Local activity summaries generated from food records: daily counts, completed meals, water entries and streaks;
  • AI memory (if enabled) and notification preferences;
  • Local JSON backups in the app’s support directory (up to 5 copies).

The current version does not collect height, weight, basal metabolism, calorie intake, diagnosis or treatment records. Preferences such as allergies, dietary and health goals — even though they live only on your device today — are treated as sensitive information.

3. What is never collected automatically

The current version sends no location, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports to third parties. Debug logs exist only in developer builds. Remote requests would only ever be made when using an enabled AI service, under a locally generated anonymous installation identifier.

4. Optional AI services

The current version (1.0.0) does not enable remote AI. If it is enabled in the future, it will only ever work the following way, after your explicit consent:

  • Photos from the camera or album may be sent to a gateway for food recognition;
  • Conversation text and minimal pantry context (item names, quantities, units and expiry status) may be sent;
  • Allergy information, health goals, profile details, AI memory, meal history, shopping history and long-term behavior records are not part of any planned remote AI requests; if that scope ever changes, we will update this policy and obtain consent as required by law;
  • No service-provider API keys are built into the app or submitted by it;
  • The gateway issues short-lived anonymous tokens bound to your local installation identifier and stores no prompts or images.

If AI-generated text, images or other content is ever provided, it will be labeled “AI-generated” in the interface so you can recognize it. In the current version, photo recognition uses Apple Vision on your device — photos never leave it.

5. QR codes

FreshLife food QR codes are parsed locally on your device; no network is used for this. Retail barcode lookup is not part of the current version.

6. Retention, export and deletion

Data stays on your device until you decide to delete it. Uninstalling the app removes its sandbox; OS backups and keychain entries follow platform behavior. At any time, in the Privacy Center, you can:

  • Export pantry, history, completed meals, shopping list and language preferences (currently as text);
  • Delete all local data, including AI memory, backups and the local identity;
  • Reset AI memory on its own.

7. Permissions

Camera, photo and notification permissions are managed by iOS. Declining optional permissions doesn’t affect manual pantry management; you can change authorizations anytime in system Settings, and the Privacy Center shows their current state.

8. Children’s privacy

FreshLife is not a service aimed at children and is not used for medical diagnosis. If we ever provide services for children, we will first complete the appropriate age rating and children’s privacy assessment.

9. Security

The app uses the iOS sandbox, standard iOS data protection and the Keychain (for the local identity); any remote communication uses HTTPS.

10. Policy updates

For important changes, we update the date on this page and, where appropriate, notify you inside the app.
