Add Food

Log an item in a few steps.

Snap, scan, pick from your library or type — use whichever feels natural. Recognition runs on your device, no API key needed; check anything you’re unsure about before saving.

{{add_methods}}

Recognition results and nutrition info are reference only: packaging labels, recall notices, and how food smells and looks come first. Retail barcode lookup isn’t available in this version.

FreshLife AI

Plan your next meal from what’s already home.

FreshLife AI starts with what is in your pantry, what needs using soon, and your preferences. On-device features work offline; remote AI is not enabled in the current version — if it ships, it will be off by default and switched on only by you.

{{ai_modes}}
What would remote AI process? Open the full data boundary

The current version (1.0.0) has no remote AI enabled. If it ships later, it will be off by default; before you enable it, the app will explain what information is processed and ask for your clear consent, and only the minimum needed for that request will be sent. Allergy information, health goals, profile details, AI memory, meal and shopping history, and long-term behavior records are not part of any planned remote AI requests. No service-provider API keys are built into the app, and the app will never ask you for one. AI suggestions are reference only — not medical advice.
