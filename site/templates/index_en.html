Vision

Getting to know your family’s rhythm.

Every household has its own rhythm: rushed weekdays, slower weekends, different tastes and dietary needs. FreshLife aims to become a personal food intelligence layer for each household—learning, with your permission, what you keep at home, how you like to eat, and how you usually shop, so everyday suggestions fit real life better.

{{vision_cards}}

These stages move in order: first make recording food easier, then build toward understanding and planning. Version 1.0 has no long-term household memory, automatic shopping or complete household food graph—those are long-term directions, not promises for today. Add Food

Log an item in a few steps.

Snap, scan, pick from your library or type — use whichever feels natural. Recognition runs on your device, no API key needed; check anything you’re unsure about before saving.

{{add_methods}}

Recognition results and nutrition info are reference only: packaging labels, recall notices, and how food smells and looks come first. Retail barcode lookup isn’t available in this version.

FreshLife AI

Plan your next meal from what’s already home.

FreshLife AI suggests from your real pantry, expiring items and preferences. On-device abilities work offline; the remote AI is not enabled in the current version — if it ships, it will be off by default and switched on only by you.

{{ai_modes}}
About the remote AI assistant: the current version (1.0.0) has no remote AI enabled. If it ships later, it will be off by default; before you enable it, the app will explain what information is processed and ask for your clear consent, and only the minimum needed for that request will be sent. Allergy information, health goals, profile details, AI memory, meal and shopping history, and long-term behavior records are not part of any planned remote AI requests. No service-provider API keys are built into the app, and the app will never ask you for one. AI suggestions are reference only — not medical advice.
