Web app · Beta

Open the Chinese-language web app for pantry, shopping and usage records. Data stays in the current browser, without App sync, camera recognition or background notifications. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

Where should I start?

Add one item with the expiry date from its packaging, a quantity and a unit. Confirm what you use or buy. FreshLife cannot automatically detect changes in your fridge.

Where are my previous records?

Check the original device, browser and entry point, then clear search filters. Home Screen and browser records may differ. Do not clear website data first. Export a backup from the original entry if records are still available; cleared local records cannot be recovered from the cloud.

Why am I still seeing an older version?

Save your work, open FreshLife while online, then close all its browser tabs and Home Screen windows and reopen it. There is no need to clear browser data.

Add FreshLife to your iPhone Home Screen

This is a web beta, not an App Store download.

  1. Open FreshLife web beta (Chinese) in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Some layouts place Share under More. Scroll or check Edit Actions if the option is missing.
  3. If offered, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  4. Open FreshLife from your Home Screen. Connect once and check offline cache readiness in Data & Backup.

The icon, name and standalone configuration are ready; physical iPhone installation has not yet been verified. Try a few records first and export backups regularly.

No native App or cross-device sync, and no background expiry notifications. If existing records do not appear, export them from the original browser and restore them in the new entry. Back up before removing the web app or clearing browser data.

Getting started

How do I add an item?

Tap the add button in the bottom-right and choose: type it, snap a photo, pick from your library, or scan. Typing is best when you want exact quantities, storage spots and expiry dates. Items with the same name, spot and expiry date merge their quantities automatically.

How is the freshness status calculated?

FreshLife compares the expiry date with today’s date and groups items into Fresh, Expiring or Expired. It recalculates across midnight, time-zone changes, and whenever the app returns to the foreground.

Photos & scanning

Why does it sometimes ask me to confirm?

When name, quantity and expiry information are reliable enough, recognition results can go straight in; when they aren’t, the app shows a short confirmation screen to keep wrong data out of your pantry. If recognition fails, you can retry or add manually — a failed attempt never loses data you’ve already saved.

Where does recognition happen?

Photo and album recognition run on your device with Apple Vision — no API key and no network. Recognition is a suggestion: you can edit the result anytime, or switch to manual entry.

Can I scan retail barcodes?

This version supports FreshLife food QR codes only; retail barcode lookup isn’t available yet.

The camera won’t work — what now?

Go to iOS Settings → FreshLife and check the camera permission. Even without camera access, you can still add items manually.

AI assistant

What’s the difference between on-device suggestions and remote AI?

Meal suggestions are computed locally from what is already in your pantry and work offline — they’re part of the current version. Remote AI is not enabled in the current version. If it ships later, it will be off by default, explain how data is processed, and ask for your clear consent before you turn it on. FreshLife will not ask you for an API key.

Will AI change my pantry on its own?

No. AI output is validated for structure and scope, and important pantry changes take effect only after you confirm them.

Why can’t I find remote AI?

Because remote AI is not enabled in the current version. Your local pantry, expiry reminders, shopping list and basic meal suggestions still work as normal. If remote AI ships later, the website and app will explain how it works and what data it processes first.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?

Core pantry and preference data live on your device, local-first. If optional remote AI ships later, only the data needed for a request would be processed, and only when you have turned it on and use it. There are no ad SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

How do I export my data?

Go to Profile → Privacy Center → Export Data. You can export pantry, history, completed meals, shopping list and language preferences (currently as text).

How do I delete my data?

In Profile → Privacy Center, you can reset AI memory or delete all local data (including backups and the local identity). Read the confirmation carefully before deleting — local data you delete may not be recoverable. For a thorough cleanup, choose “Delete all data” first and then uninstall the app.
