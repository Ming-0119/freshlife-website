Getting started

How do I add an item?

Tap the add button in the bottom-right and choose: type it, snap a photo, pick from your library, or scan. Typing is best when you want exact quantities, storage spots and expiry dates. Items with the same name, spot and expiry date merge their quantities automatically.

How is the freshness status calculated?

FreshLife compares the expiry date with today’s date and groups items into Fresh, Expiring or Expired. It recalculates across midnight, time-zone changes, and whenever the app returns to the foreground.

Photos & scanning

Why does it sometimes ask me to confirm?

When name, quantity and expiry information are reliable enough, recognition results can go straight in; when they aren’t, the app shows a short confirmation screen to keep wrong data out of your pantry. If recognition fails, you can retry or add manually — a failed attempt never loses data you’ve already saved.

Where does recognition happen?

Photo and album recognition run on your device with Apple Vision — no API key and no network. Recognition is a suggestion: you can edit the result anytime, or switch to manual entry.

Can I scan retail barcodes?

This version supports FreshLife food QR codes only; retail barcode lookup isn’t available yet.

The camera won’t work — what now?

Go to iOS Settings → FreshLife and check the camera permission. Even without camera access, you can still add items manually.

AI assistant

What’s the difference between on-device suggestions and remote AI?

Meal suggestions are computed locally from your real pantry data and work offline — they’re part of the current version. The remote AI assistant needs a network and a gateway; it’s off in the current version, needs your explicit consent to third-party processing before it can be enabled, and never asks you for an API key.

Will AI change my pantry on its own?

No. AI output is validated for structure and scope; important changes go through FreshLife’s regular data flow and are confirmed by you, so you always see the result and feedback.

Remote AI isn’t working — what should I do?

Check your network connection and the AI authorization state in the app, then try again. While remote services are unavailable, your local pantry, expiry reminders, shopping and basic meal suggestions keep working normally.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?

Core pantry and preference data live on your device, local-first. Only when you actively use an optional AI feature you’ve allowed would the data needed for that request be processed. There are no ad SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

How do I export my data?

Go to Profile → Privacy Center → Export Data. You can export pantry, history, completed meals, shopping list and language preferences (currently as text).

How do I delete my data?

In Profile → Privacy Center, you can reset AI memory or delete all local data (including backups and the local identity). Read the confirmation carefully before deleting — local data you delete may not be recoverable. For a thorough cleanup, choose “Delete all data” first and then uninstall the app.
