FreshLifeHousehold food decisions · iPhone and iPad

What should we eat today?
Don’t start from scratch.

FreshLife keeps track of what you have, what to use first, and what you’re missing—then helps you plan the next meal from what is already at home. It is more than an inventory list: it takes some of the repetition out of everyday food decisions. Core features run on your device. No account needed.

Android 1.0.1 · Submitted for Huawei review

Your pantry, kept on your device.

Track food, record consumption and waste, and move purchased items into your pantry. No account is needed for this offline release.

  • Pantry, shopping lists and local expiry reminders.
  • Local household settings and usage estimates based on confirmed records.
  • Backup export, restore and privacy controls.

No cloud sync, online store or membership transactions in this release. Screenshots show the real Chinese-language Android 1.0.1 build running in an emulator.

Android guide (Chinese)

A simple daily routine

  1. 01 · Add your food

    Record the item, quantity, storage location and expiry date so your pantry matches what you have.

  2. 02 · Use it and confirm

    Review what is nearing expiry. Confirm consumption or waste when it happens to keep records useful.

  3. 03 · Plan your next shop

    Keep what you need in a shopping list and add purchases to your pantry. Export a backup before changing phones.

Can my family share the same pantry?

Household settings are stored on this device. They do not create a shared account or synchronize records between family members’ phones.

How should I read usage estimates?

Estimates depend on the records you confirm. With too little history, keep recording instead of treating an estimate as a purchase instruction.

What happens when I change phones?

This offline release does not offer cloud recovery. Export a backup while your old device is available, then use the restore option. Local reminders also need notification permission.

FreshLife AI

Plan your next meal from what’s already home.

FreshLife AI starts with what is in your pantry, what needs using soon, and your preferences. On-device features work offline; remote AI is not enabled in the current version — if it ships, it will be off by default and switched on only by you.

On-device recognition & suggestions Works on-device

Camera and photo-library recognition use Apple Vision on your device; meal suggestions are computed locally from what is already in your pantry and work offline.

Optional remote AI assistant Remote feature not enabled

Not enabled in the current version. If it ships later, it will be off by default, switched on by you, and will send only the minimum needed for your request.

Boundaries & confirmation Built into version 1.0

AI recognizes and suggests — it never changes your pantry on its own. Recognition results, meal plans and shopping changes all need your confirmation first.

What would remote AI process? Open the full data boundary

The current version (1.0.0) has no remote AI enabled. If it ships later, it will be off by default; before you enable it, the app will explain what information is processed and ask for your clear consent, and only the minimum needed for that request will be sent. Allergy information, health goals, profile details, AI memory, meal and shopping history, and long-term behavior records are not part of any planned remote AI requests. No service-provider API keys are built into the app, and the app will never ask you for one. AI suggestions are reference only — not medical advice.

FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do I have to create an account to use it?

No. Pantry, reminders, meals and shopping all work out of the box, and your profile stays on your device. Accounts and cloud sync are planned capabilities — they’re not in the current version.

Does it work without a network?

Yes. Pantry, expiry reminders, meal planning and everything you’ve saved keep working offline. Remote AI is a future optional capability; it isn’t enabled in the current version.

How reliable is photo recognition?

Recognition runs on-device with Apple Vision and counts as a suggestion. When the app isn’t sure, it asks you to check — and you can always edit the result or type the item yourself. This version doesn’t support retail barcode lookup.

Will AI change my pantry on its own?

No. AI only recognizes and suggests; it never changes your pantry directly. Recognition results, meal plans and shopping changes all take effect only after you confirm them.

Can I fully trust expiry dates and nutrition info?

No. Dates and nutrition come from your entry, packaging or recognition — they’re management references. Packaging labels, recall notices, and how food smells and looks come first.

Will FreshLife gradually learn my family’s habits?

Local preferences are different from cross-device household memory. Android 1.0.1 includes local household settings and usage estimates based on confirmed records; iOS capabilities depend on its version. Full cloud household memory is not available, and the app does not place orders automatically. Any future online service will explain its data use and request the necessary consent before launch.

How do I export or delete my data?

In the app, go to Profile → Privacy Center. You can export text data, reset AI memory, or delete all local data. Deleted local data may not be recoverable.
