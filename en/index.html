What should we eat today?
Don’t start from scratch.

FreshLife keeps track of what you have, what to use first, and what you’re missing—then helps you plan the next meal from what is already at home. It is more than an inventory list: it takes some of the repetition out of everyday food decisions. Core features run on your device. No account needed.

Why FreshLife

Small moments saved, every day.

FreshLife isn’t a complicated kitchen manager. It does three small things: remembers for you, reminds you, and gives you an answer when you need one.

Know what’s at home

No more staring into the fridge: what you bought, when, and how long it keeps — all remembered on your device.

A timely reminder before food is due

No nagging—just one useful reminder when an ingredient is worth using soon.

Your data stays on your device

Vision

Getting to know your family’s rhythm.

Every household has its own rhythm: rushed weekdays, slower weekends, different tastes and dietary needs. FreshLife aims to become a personal food intelligence layer for each household—learning, with your permission, what you keep at home, how you like to eat, and how you usually shop, so everyday suggestions fit real life better.

Now · In the app today

Keep each day straight

A local pantry, expiry reminders, meal ideas and a shopping list — first, make today clear. This is everything FreshLife does today, and the foundation it builds on.

Next · Not shipped yet

Learn your family’s rhythm

Gradually learn your household’s tastes, dietary restrictions and usual buys, so suggestions fit better. Memory stays on your device—explained first and enabled by you.

Long term · Direction

A food intelligence layer for your home

Understand a whole year’s rhythm — seasons, habits, budget — and suggest the right thing at the right time. A direction, not today’s feature.

These stages move in order: first make recording food easier, then build toward understanding and planning. Version 1.0 has no long-term household memory, automatic shopping or complete household food graph—those are long-term directions, not promises for today. FreshLife AI

Plan your next meal from what’s already home.

FreshLife AI suggests from your real pantry, expiring items and preferences. On-device abilities work offline; the remote AI is not enabled in the current version — if it ships, it will be off by default and switched on only by you.

Optional remote AI assistant Needs network

Not enabled in the current version. If it ships later, it will be off by default, switched on by you, and will send only the minimum needed for your request.

Boundaries & confirmation Available today

AI recognizes and suggests — it never changes your pantry on its own. Recognition results, meal plans and shopping changes all need your confirmation first.

About the remote AI assistant: the current version (1.0.0) has no remote AI enabled. If it ships later, it will be off by default; before you enable it, the app will explain what information is processed and ask for your clear consent, and only the minimum needed for that request will be sent. Allergy information, health goals, profile details, AI memory, meal and shopping history, and long-term behavior records are not part of any planned remote AI requests. No service-provider API keys are built into the app, and the app will never ask you for one. AI suggestions are reference only — not medical advice.

FAQ

Questions you might have.

Do I have to create an account to use it?

No. Pantry, reminders, meals and shopping all work out of the box, and your profile stays on your device. Accounts and cloud sync are planned capabilities — they’re not in the current version.

Does it work without a network?

Yes. Pantry, expiry reminders, meal planning and everything you’ve saved keep working offline. Remote AI is a future optional capability; it isn’t enabled in the current version.

How reliable is photo recognition?

Recognition runs on-device with Apple Vision and counts as a suggestion. When the app isn’t sure, it asks you to check — and you can always edit the result or type the item yourself. This version doesn’t support retail barcode lookup.

Will AI change my pantry on its own?

No. AI only recognizes and suggests; it never changes your pantry directly. Recognition results, meal plans and shopping changes all take effect only after you confirm them.

Can I fully trust expiry dates and nutrition info?

No. Dates and nutrition come from your entry, packaging or recognition — they’re management references. Packaging labels, recall notices, and how food smells and looks come first.

Will FreshLife gradually learn my family’s habits?

That’s a long-term direction, not a feature of the current version. Today’s FreshLife has no long-term memory and does no automatic shopping. If memory or automatic-suggestion features ever ship, they’ll be off by default, explained first, used only with your consent, and kept on your device. We won’t “learn you” silently in the background.

How do I export or delete my data?

In the app, go to Profile → Privacy Center. You can export text data, reset AI memory, or delete all local data. Deleted local data may not be recoverable.
