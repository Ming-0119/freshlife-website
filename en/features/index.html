Pantry & expiry reminders

What you have, and when it runs out — at a glance.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

Keep what you have, where it lives and when it expires in one place. A gentle nudge before the date helps prevent duplicate buys and food being forgotten.

How to use it

  • Add food by photo, album, QR scan or by typing;
  • Items are grouped automatically into “Fresh / Expiring / Expired”;
  • iOS local notifications remind you before the date — lead time is yours to adjust;
  • Log used, tossed, donated or frozen in one tap, and your pantry stays current.

What to watch for

Expiry dates come from your entry, packaging or recognition — they’re a management reference. Whether food is actually safe to eat depends on packaging, storage and how it looks and smells.

Current status

Pantry management, expiry grouping and local reminders are all in the current version (1.0.0) and run on your device — no network needed.

Four ways to add food

Snap, pick, scan or type — your call.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

Want it quick? Snap a photo. Want it exact? Type it. Choose whichever of the four methods fits the moment.

How to use it

  • Snap a photo: on-device Apple Vision tries to read the name, category and date;
  • Pick from your library: recognize a photo you’ve already taken and add it;
  • Scan a QR code: scan a FreshLife food QR code to bring the item in quickly;
  • Type it yourself: enter name, quantity, spot and expiry date — most reliable, most flexible.

What to watch for

Recognition is a suggestion: when it isn’t sure, the app asks you to check. Camera and photo permissions are requested only when used, and refusing them doesn’t stop manual entry. Retail barcode lookup isn’t available in this version.

Current status

All four methods are in the current version. Camera and photo-library recognition run on your device — no API key or network needed.

Meals & cooking

See what you can cook with what’s on hand.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

Answer “what’s for dinner”: meal ideas built from your current and expiring ingredients, expiring items first — with step-by-step timers while you cook.

How to use it

  • Generate local meal plans on the Meals screen — works offline;
  • Missing ingredients are flagged and can go straight to your shopping list;
  • Pick a plan and enter cooking mode with step-by-step timers;
  • Finishing a meal consumes its ingredients, so your records stay consistent.

What to watch for

Meal ideas are suggestions, not medical or nutritional prescriptions. Nutrition and calorie data come from general databases or recognition and may be off; for allergies and special diets, trust the packaging and professional advice.

Current status

Local meal planning and cooking mode are in the current version; suggestions are computed on-device, no network needed.

Shopping list

Everything missing, in one list.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

When your pantry runs low or a recipe is missing something, FreshLife suggests a restock — easier to check while shopping, fewer forgotten items and fewer double buys.

How to use it

  • Open the shopping list from Explore to see restock suggestions;
  • Tick what you bought and the list tidies itself;
  • When you’re home, add the new items by photo or by hand — the loop closes.

What to watch for

Restock suggestions come from your pantry data and are references, not orders — what and how much to buy is always your call. The list lives on your device.

Current status

The shopping list and restock suggestions are in the current version.

iPhone & iPad

Quick to log on iPhone, easy to plan on iPad.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

FreshLife fits both iPhone and iPad: quick logging on your phone, wide-layout reviewing and planning on your iPad. No account needed.

How to use it

  • iPhone: five bottom tabs keep logging and reminders at hand;
  • iPad: a sidebar switches quickly between Today, Pantry, Meals, Explore and Profile;
  • Both devices share the same information structure; data is stored on each device separately.

What to watch for

The current version has no cloud sync — data doesn’t move between devices on its own. Multi-device sync is a “Planned” capability.

Current status

The universal iPhone and iPad app is in the current version; cloud sync and accounts are planned, not shipped.

Privacy & data control

Data stays on your device; export or delete anytime.

Built into version 1.0

What it helps with

Pantry, preferences and other core data live only on your device. No account, no ad SDKs, no cross-app tracking — and you can export or delete at any time.

How to use it

  • Profile → Privacy Center: export text data, reset AI memory, or delete all local data;
  • Camera, photo and notification permissions are requested only when used, with an explanation;
  • You can change any permission later in iOS Settings.

What to watch for

Deleted local data may not be recoverable; uninstalling the app removes the data in its sandbox. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Current status

Local storage, export and deletion are all in the current version. Remote AI isn’t enabled yet; if it ships, it stays off by default and needs your clear consent.

FreshLife AI

On-device today; remote AI not enabled yet.

Works on-device

What it helps with

Recognize ingredients and suggest meals from what is already in your pantry. AI only recognizes and suggests — it never changes your pantry on its own.

How to use it

  • On-device recognition: photos and album recognition use Apple Vision on your device;
  • Local suggestions: meal ideas are computed locally from your pantry and work offline;
  • Remote AI: if it ships later, it will be off by default, switched on by you, with clear consent and only the minimum sent for each request.

What to watch for

AI suggestions are reference only — not medical advice. Recognition and nutrition data can be off; for important decisions, trust packaging and professionals. Any AI-generated content, if offered in the future, will be labeled “AI-generated” in the interface.

Current status

On-device recognition and local suggestions are in the current version (1.0.0); the remote AI assistant isn’t enabled and is a planned, optional capability. Recognition results, meal plans and shopping changes all take effect only after you confirm them.
